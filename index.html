Broke down?

  1. Get off the road if you can, hazards on. On the interstate, stay in the vehicle with your seatbelt on.
  2. If anyone is hurt or you're in a dangerous spot, call 911 first.
  3. Call us. During hours we send the tow and hold a bay. After hours, the line forwards to our towing partner, who answers around the clock.
  4. Tell them "Six Point" and they'll bring it straight to our lot. We start on it first thing.
